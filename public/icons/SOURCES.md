The Minecraft PNGs are `icons/icon_128x128.png` and
`icons/snapshot/icon_128x128.png` from the official Java Edition 1.20.1 asset
index, resolved through https://piston-meta.mojang.com/mc/game/version_manifest_v2.json.
The URL hashes were verified against each downloaded file's SHA-1.
They are the modern release/snapshot icons described at
https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/our-icons-are-changing,
used consistently across versions rather than recreating historical icon changes.
